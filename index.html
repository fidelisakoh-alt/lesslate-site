How it works

One clear job: help you get out of the door.

01

Choose your leave-by time

Tell LessLate when you need to be out of the door.

02

Get the reminder

LessLate alerts you when it is time to go, even when your phone is locked.

03

Leave, or hear it again

If you are still there, LessLate reminds you again. Once you leave, the reminder ends.

Private by design

Your location stays on your iPhone.

LessLate has no accounts, adverts or analytics. Location is used on-device to detect whether you have left. It is not sent to us or sold to anyone.
