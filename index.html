More help and support ↗
What is LessLate?

LessLate is a departure reminder app for iPhone. Choose when to leave, get an alarm, and receive follow up reminders if you have not left your saved starting area. It is for everyday plans when knowing the time does not always turn into getting out the door.

Does the alarm stop when I leave?

Stopping an alarm starts the departure check. Once LessLate confirms that you have left the starting area, that journey’s active reminder and follow ups end. You can also end a journey yourself if your plans change.

What counts as leaving?

LessLate looks for two consecutive accurate location readings more than 50 metres from your saved starting point. Always Location access and Precise Location are needed for departure checks while your iPhone is locked or you are using another app.

Is LessLate free?

Manual Leave by reminders, departure detection and Momentum Mode are free. One automatic weekly routine is also included. Pro adds Arrive by planning, saved trips, more automatic routines and more manual reminder intervals.

Does it work without an internet connection?

The core manual alarm and departure check can work without an internet connection, with the required permissions and a usable location signal. Map searches, journey estimates, purchases and restoration need a connection.
