01

Make your plan

Choose when you need to leave. LessLate captures a fresh, precise starting location when you commit, so you don’t have to reset it for every reminder.

Get ready. Then go.

Preparation notifications help you keep track of the time. Your alarm signals when to leave. Still at your starting area? Follow-up alerts help you keep moving.

Make it out the door

LessLate confirms departure using two accurate location readings beyond 50 metres from your starting point. The active reminder then ends automatically.

Less mental maths Pro

Know when to arrive?
Work back from there.

Choose your destination and arrival time. Smart Leave Time uses Apple Maps travel estimates to work out when to go, with time to get ready shown in your plan.

  • Travel your way

    Driving, public transport, walking or cycling, where estimates are available. Review the calculated leave time before you commit.

  • Keep your regular trips

    Save routines so the next plan takes less effort. Applying a routine uses a fresh starting location and travel estimate.

  • Choose your pressure

    Gentle every five minutes. Focused every two. Strong every minute. Pick the follow-up rhythm that suits you.

Travel times are estimates, not a guarantee of arrival. LessLate does not continuously monitor traffic in the background.
