The short versionNo LessLate account. No adverts or cross-app advertising tracking. Reminder records stay in the app’s local storage. Apple processes map and route requests. RevenueCat processes subscription information and purchase reporting.

1. Who operates LessLate

LessLate is operated by Fidelis Akoh in the United Kingdom. Fidelis Akoh is the controller responsible for the personal information processed to operate LessLate. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean that operator, not a limited company.

Contact: lesslateapp@gmail.com.

2. Information on your iPhone

The app stores reminder records, starting-area details, saved routines, travel plans, departure history and preferences locally. It uses precise location readings to compare your position with the starting point of an active reminder. LessLate does not send those reminder records, routines, history or departure-detection readings to a LessLate server or to RevenueCat.

When you commit a reminder, the app requires a fresh, sufficiently accurate starting position. During an active reminder, location monitoring can operate in the background to recognise departure. Ending the reminder ends the monitoring associated with it. Ending a reminder does not necessarily delete saved history or routines.

LessLate has no account-based cloud sync. Your device or computer backup settings may separately include app data.

3. Apple Maps and travel estimates

The map, destination search and Pro travel estimates use Apple Maps through MapKit. These features send relevant requests to Apple, including the visible map area, search text and regional hints. Travel estimates also use origin and destination coordinates, travel mode and arrival or departure timing.

This is separate from on-device departure detection. We therefore do not claim that every use of your location stays on your phone. Apple processes these requests under its own Apple Maps privacy information.

We use the requested location and journey information to provide the reminder and route-planning features you choose. You control location access in iPhone Settings.

4. Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple handles App Store payments, account information, renewals and transactions. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

We use RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore them and determine whether Pro access is active. The SDK sends an automatically generated app-user identifier, purchase or receipt information, subscription status and relevant app/device metadata, such as platform, app version, locale and currency.

LessLate does not supply RevenueCat with a name, email address, custom account identifier, location, reminder, routine or departure history. Its generated identifier is not a LessLate login. It still allows subscription records to be associated over time.

RevenueCat also provides purchase analytics, including subscription activity, renewals and revenue reporting. We use these reports to understand how Pro is performing. There is no separate app-usage analytics or advertising SDK in LessLate.

RevenueCat acts as our processor for this subscription service. See its privacy policy and Data Processing Addendum. We do not sell personal information or use this information for cross-app advertising tracking.

Our lawful basis for providing and restoring paid access is performance of our contract with you. Fraud prevention, service reliability and proportionate purchase reporting rely on our legitimate interests in operating and understanding the service.

5. Permissions and Lock Screen information

Alarm permission enables the scheduled alarm. Notification permission enables preparation messages. Always Location access and Precise Location support departure detection while the app is in the background or your phone is locked.

Location permission can be requested when you set a reminder or choose “Use My Current Location”. Using that map action does not itself set an alarm.

Live Activities and notifications may show reminder information on your Lock Screen. Use the app’s destination-privacy preference to hide the destination where offered. You can also control notifications, Live Activities and location permissions in iPhone Settings. Disabling a permission can limit its related feature.

Permission to access a device feature is separate from the legal basis for processing subscription and support information.

6. Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and anything you attach. Diagnostics are shared only when you choose to send them. Review screenshots and logs before sending them, and avoid including home addresses, payment details or other information we do not need.

We use support information to answer questions and investigate faults, based on our legitimate interest in supporting the app. Our support email uses Google’s email service.

This website has no account, contact form, advertising tracker or analytics script. It does not set advertising or analytics cookies or load external fonts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process IP addresses and technical request information to deliver and secure it. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Google’s privacy policy.

7. Retention, sharing and transfers

Local history and saved routines remain available until removed through available app controls or by deleting the app’s local data. Device backups may retain separate copies. Uninstalling LessLate does not cancel an Apple subscription or automatically erase Apple’s or RevenueCat’s subscription records.

Subscription records are retained as needed to provide access and restoration, prevent fraud, resolve disputes and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Support correspondence is kept for as long as needed to resolve the enquiry and related follow-up. Retention is assessed against those purposes rather than the life of an active reminder.

We use the providers described above, and may disclose information where required by law. Some providers process information outside the UK, including in the United States. RevenueCat’s published DPA includes standard contractual clauses and the UK transfer addendum for relevant restricted transfers. Other providers describe their safeguards in their privacy terms. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your request.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact lesslateapp@gmail.com. We may need enough information to locate a subscription record and verify that a request is yours. We do not collect extra identity information solely to make otherwise unidentifiable records identifiable.

We cannot remotely access or delete reminder and location records held only on your device. Subscription cancellation is handled separately through Apple; see Subscriptions & terms.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. LessLate does not make legal or similarly significant decisions about you through automated profiling.
