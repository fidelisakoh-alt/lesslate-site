Free and Pro

LessLate Free

Manual Leave by reminders, departure detection, the starting area map, Momentum Mode, Momentum Run and history are free. Manual follow ups every 2 minutes are included. Today and one automatic weekly routine are also included.

LessLate Pro

Pro adds Arrive by with Smart Leave, available Apple Maps journey estimates, Saved trips, the ability to create more automatic weekly routines, and manual repeat intervals of every minute or every 5 minutes.

Automatic weekly routines use a 5 minute follow up interval. They are separate from Saved trips, which are reusable Arrive by plans that you review and set manually.

Plans, pricing and payment

LessLate Pro is an auto renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly. The app displays the available plans, local prices and billing periods before you confirm a purchase. App Store pricing controls the amount you pay; no website price or promotion is promised.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A pending purchase does not unlock Pro until the store confirms an active entitlement. Purchase availability can vary by account or region.

Renewal and cancellation

Your subscription renews automatically for the same period unless you turn off auto renewal at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LessLate to manage or cancel. You can also use Manage Subscription in LessLate’s subscription details. Apple explains how to cancel a subscription.

Cancellation stops the next renewal. Pro normally remains available until the end of the paid period. Deleting LessLate does not cancel the subscription. Apple handles refund requests through Report a Problem, subject to its rules and applicable law.

If Pro ends

Free reminders and departure detection remain available. Every existing automatic weekly routine remains available to view, edit, pause, resume and delete. Pro is required to create another automatic routine when you already have the free allowance of one.

New Arrive by plans, Saved trips controls and selecting additional manual reminder intervals require active Pro access. An already armed reminder is not rewritten just because access changes.

Restore Purchases

No LessLate account is required. Subscription access is restored through the Apple Account used for the App Store purchase. Open the Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use an internet connection and the same Apple Account.

RevenueCat helps validate purchases and manage entitlement. If restoration fails, follow the purchase support steps. A temporary store problem does not remove free reminder features.
