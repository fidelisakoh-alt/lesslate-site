LessLate Pro

Monthly: £4.99 for one month.
Yearly: £39.99 for one year, charged as the full annual amount.

These are UK prices. Apple displays the price for your storefront before you confirm a purchase. Subscriptions will be available with the public App Store launch.

Pro adds Arrive by and Smart Leave Time, Apple Maps travel estimates, saved and recurring routine controls, and Gentle or Strong pressure choices. Core Leave by alarms, departure detection, Focused follow-ups, Momentum Mode and Momentum Run remain in Free.

Payment, renewal and cancellation

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. It renews for the selected period unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Check Apple’s purchase confirmation for the applicable price and terms.

Manage or cancel through Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LessLate, or use Manage Subscription in the app. Cancellation stops future renewal; Pro normally remains available until the paid period ends. Deleting LessLate does not cancel your subscription.

When access expires, your saved data is preserved and an already-active reminder keeps its settings. Creating, editing and applying Pro routines or using other Pro controls requires active access.

Restoration, pending purchases and refunds

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. If a purchase is pending approval, Pro becomes available after the purchase completes and active access is confirmed. Do not repeatedly purchase while awaiting approval.

Apple handles App Store refund requests. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; eligibility is determined by Apple and applicable consumer law. For a subscription-access problem, contact lesslateapp@gmail.com.

Terms of Use

The app uses Apple’s Standard End User Licence Agreement. App Store purchases are also subject to Apple’s applicable store terms. Our privacy policy explains how information is handled.

Travel times are estimates. Location accuracy, permissions and device conditions can affect reminder and departure behaviour. The five-minute Momentum Run allowance does not extend your travel estimate or guarantee arrival. LessLate is not intended for emergency or safety-critical use.

Nothing on this page limits your statutory consumer rights.
