Your first reminder

  1. Choose Leave by and the time you want to leave. With Pro, choose Arrive by to plan from a destination and arrival time.
  2. Review the plan and tap the commitment button showing your leave time.
  3. Follow the alarm and location permission steps. Departure detection needs Always location access and Precise Location. Allow notifications if you want preparation messages.
  4. Confirm the app shows your reminder as set. If it shows an error, resolve it before relying on the reminder.

LessLate captures a fresh starting position when you commit. You do not need to tap “Use My Current Location” before every reminder.

Reminders and departure

When do preparation notifications arrive?

LessLate schedules upcoming preparation cues at 30, 15 and 5 minutes before the leave time where those times are still in the future. A getting-ready cue can also reflect your selected preparation time. Coinciding cues may be combined.

These are ordinary notifications, separate from the alarm. Notification permission, Focus and notification settings can affect how they appear or sound. Setting a reminder close to departure does not send all the earlier cues.

How often does it remind me again?

Free includes Focused follow-ups every two minutes. Pro also offers Gentle every five minutes and Strong every minute. Older reminders without a saved pressure may retain their five-minute interval. The active reminder ends after departure is confirmed or you end it.

How does LessLate know I have left?

It requires two accurate location readings beyond 50 metres from the stored starting point. Readings must meet the app’s accuracy check. Confirmation can take longer where GPS is poor, such as indoors or between tall buildings.

The starting point stays fixed for that active reminder. It does not move along with you.

What does the five-minute window mean?

A confirmed departure up to five minutes after the planned leave time still qualifies towards Momentum Run. The actual confirmation time stays in your result.

The window does not delay your alarm or guarantee an on-time arrival. Allow for the journey and for moving beyond the 50-metre starting area.

How do I cancel or end a reminder?

Use Cancel Reminder before departure, or End reminder / End for Today where shown, and confirm your choice. Check the resulting status. A cancelled or ended reminder is not counted as a successful departure.

If the app warns that an alarm may still sound, do not assume cancellation succeeded. Follow the recovery guidance and contact support if needed.

My alarm did not sound, or the reminder needs attention.

Open LessLate and read the reminder status. Check alarm permission. A Live Activity card alone is not proof that an audible alarm sounded.

Use Restore reminder if offered, then confirm a new alert is actually set. If the reminder was not set, return to Setup and create a new one. If recovery or cancellation fails, copy Support Diagnostics before reinstalling. Reinstalling can erase the evidence and your local data.

For an important appointment, use a separate backup alarm while investigating a fault.

Starting location and permissions

Why does LessLate need Always and Precise Location?

Departure detection needs sufficiently accurate readings while a reminder is active, including in the background or with the phone locked. In iPhone Settings, find LessLate, open Location, choose Always and enable Precise Location.

Location Services must also be enabled on the device. Return to the app and follow its current guidance.

What does “Use My Current Location” do?

It requests permission if needed and refreshes the Starting area map. It does not set an alarm. Moving the map is for viewing the area, not selecting a different departure point.

When you commit a new reminder, LessLate checks for a fresh accurate position. It should not silently reuse the previous reminder’s location if that check fails.

Does my location stay on the phone?

Departure detection is processed on your iPhone. Map display, destination searches and travel estimates send relevant requests to Apple Maps, including coordinates where needed. RevenueCat does not receive your location from LessLate. Read the privacy policy for the distinction.

What changes when I apply a routine somewhere else?

LessLate captures a fresh starting point and recalculates the travel estimate. If the calculated leave time changes by a minute or more during that process, the revised plan is shown for review instead of being silently armed.

Live Activities

Why is there no card for tomorrow’s reminder?

The app-owned Live Activity starts only within four hours of the planned leave time. If you set a reminder further ahead, open the app again inside that window to let it start. Automatic background activation is not guaranteed.

The alarm is scheduled separately. The absence of a Live Activity does not itself mean the alarm is missing.

The reminder is soon, but no Live Activity appears.

Allow Live Activities when iOS asks. Check Settings → Apps → LessLate → Live Activities, and the Lock Screen access settings under Face ID & Passcode. Names can vary by iOS version.

Open LessLate and check the Reminder Live Activity row in Support Diagnostics. iOS can limit or remove activities, and an app-owned activity cannot stay active indefinitely.

Can I hide my destination on the Lock Screen?

Enable Hide destination on Lock Screen where offered in the app. Check your iPhone’s notification-preview settings too. Reminder times can remain visible even when the destination is hidden.

Purchases and Pro

I paid, but Pro is not active.

Check your internet connection and tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. “Nothing to restore” means the app did not find active access through that restoration attempt.

If Apple shows an active subscription but LessLate still shows Free, contact support with the app version and relevant diagnostics. Do not buy again to resolve the mismatch.

How do I cancel? What happens to my data?

Open Manage Subscription in the app, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LessLate. Turning off renewal normally keeps Pro available until the paid period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Losing Pro preserves saved routines, plans and history. An already-active reminder keeps its stored pressure. Pro-only controls become unavailable. Read Subscriptions & terms.

What should I send to support?

Include your app version and build, iPhone model, iOS version, expected leave time, what happened and whether the phone was locked. Copy Support Diagnostics, including Alarm observed and Last error when relevant.

Review the contents first. Remove personal photos, addresses, coordinates or account details you do not want to share. Do not uninstall before saving the information.
